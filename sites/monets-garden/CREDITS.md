# Artwork, sources and dependency notices

## Claude Monet — Bridge over a Pond of Water Lilies, 1899

Oil on canvas, 92.7 × 73.7 cm. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, 29.100.113.
H. O. Havemeyer Collection, Bequest of Mrs. H. O. Havemeyer, 1929.

- Collection account: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/437127
- Reproduction / CC0 declaration: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bridge_over_a_Pond_of_Water_Lilies_MET_DT1854.jpg
- Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/53/Bridge_over_a_Pond_of_Water_Lilies_MET_DT1854.jpg

`monet-garden.webp` is a resized, border-trimmed derivative. The preview, canopy,
pond, continuous foliage passages, ground passage and lily/willow masks derive
from this same reproduction. Only mask boundaries, depth placement and the
identified ground/bridge treatments are adapted; vegetation retains grouped
source brushwork. The path texture samples the painting's palette.

## Painter and portrait

`monet-painting-cutout.webp` is a masked crop of John Singer Sargent's
**Claude Monet Painting by the Edge of a Wood (1885)**, a public-domain painting.
Its use is identified in the page as a collage, not a record of Monet's 1899 session.

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sargent_MonetPainting.jpg
- https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7c/Sargent_MonetPainting.jpg

`monet-1899.webp` depicts Monet photographed by Nadar in 1899. Historical image
and reproduction status are documented on the Commons file page:

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Claude_Monet_1899_Nadar_crop.jpg
- https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a4/Claude_Monet_1899_Nadar_crop.jpg
