## UI icons

The six glyphs in `icons.js` come from [Lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide): arrow-left, arrow-right, rotate-ccw, x, external-link, and book-open. They are assembled into the local registry without a runtime CDN dependency.

Lucide uses the ISC License; Feather-derived icons are covered by MIT. The complete upstream notice, including the Feather list and copyrights, is retained in [licenses/lucide-LICENSE.txt](./licenses/lucide-LICENSE.txt).
